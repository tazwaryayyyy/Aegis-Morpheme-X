## 🇧🇩 Dhaka: Extreme Need Environment

### Environmental Challenges
- **AQI 246** → "Very Unhealthy" (WHO guideline: 15)
- **Life expectancy -6.9 years** from air pollution
- **Monsoon flooding** affects 25% of population annually
- **Population density**: 47,400 people/km² (one of world's densest)

### Health Crisis Indicators
- **High HPAI risk** in poultry markets
- **Limited healthcare access**: 1 doctor per 5,000 people
- **Informal economy**: 70% without health insurance
- **Climate vulnerability**: Top 10 most climate-at-risk cities
